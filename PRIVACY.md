# Privacy

Copyright © 2026 2351485 ALBERTA INC.

PDFStruct processes documents **only on your machine**:

- No uploads, no accounts, no telemetry, no analytics, no ad networks.
- Your PDFs and the extracted text/outline never leave your computer
  unless you yourself send them somewhere.
- The local API listens on `127.0.0.1` by default; the browser
  extension talks only to that local API.
- Outputs are ordinary files written where you direct them
  (`extracted_<name>/` by default). **Data export** is reading those
  files; **data deletion** is deleting those files — there is no
  server copy to export or erase, because there is no server.

The only external links in the project are the voluntary tip links in
`.github/FUNDING.yml` (Buy Me a Coffee, PayPal), which load nothing and
track nothing inside the tool.
