About PDFStruct

  
Version 0.2.0. Decomposition, the knowledgebank,
  and talk-to-the-page all run on this device, in this browser.
  Your documents are never uploaded. The model catalogue and
  licence validation are the only features that use the network,
  and only when you ask them to: models download to your machine;
  validation sends a licence key and the product id, never a
  document.
